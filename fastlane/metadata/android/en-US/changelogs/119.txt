- Pop-up player can use a Foldex window you can resize and place freely (Settings → Video)
- Pop-up window: play/pause in the center, rewind/fast-forward on the sides
- Skip amount in seconds or % of the video length
- Videos resume where you stopped ("Start over" to restart)
- Playback speed button (0.5x–2x)
- Double-tap left/right to rewind/fast-forward
- Swipe up/down for brightness (left) or volume (right)
