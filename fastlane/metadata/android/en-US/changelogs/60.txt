1.5.0 (New)
- Properties now shows a lot more detail
- Images: resolution, megapixels, aspect ratio, and EXIF (date taken, camera, aperture, shutter, ISO, focal length, location)
- Video/audio: duration, resolution, bitrate, format, title, artist
- Folders: number of files and folders, plus total size
- Files: extension, MIME type, permissions (rwx)
- "Compute hashes" shows MD5 and SHA-256, calculated only when you ask
- Long-press any row to copy its value
