1.14.13
- The pop-up player can now use a Foldex window (Settings → Video). Resize it freely and place it anywhere. The first time, allow display over other apps
- The pop-up window has play/pause in the center and rewind/fast-forward on each side (the Android standard pop-up too, on devices that allow it)
- Choose how far fast-forward and rewind jump, in seconds or as a share (%) of the video length (Settings → Video → Skip amount)
- Videos resume where you stopped last time (tap "Start over" at the top to go back to the beginning)
- Change the playback speed of videos with the "1x" button at the top (0.5x to 2x)
- Double-tap the left or right side of a video to rewind or fast-forward (keep tapping to skip more)
- Swipe up or down on a video to change brightness (left half) or volume (right half)
